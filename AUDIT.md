# Midsummer Milano: homepage audit and competitor gaps

Benchmarked against the way leading luxury bed houses present themselves online (Hästens,
Savoir Beds, Vispring, Treca Paris).

## The homepage today

| | |
|---|---|
| **Strong** | Photography that is the house's own. A calm sequence of chapters (words, craft, inside the bed, materials, why, longevity, recognition, journal, Instagram, letters, the atelier), each with one clear next step. A single appointment action that is always one tap away. Proof that is earned (Green Product Award 2025, press) rather than claimed. Structured data and an llms.txt summary, so search engines and AI assistants can describe the house correctly. |
| **Fixed in this round** | The founder line removed from the opening. "Two artisans. One bed." now shows the seamstress at work; "Only what nature makes" shows a Loro Piana throw. The layer drawing replaced by *Three parts, made as one*, which shows the craft without the build. The spring-layer stat replaced by "100% natural fibres". A newsletter ("Letters from the atelier") added. |
| **Still to win** | 1. **Clients' voices.** Competitors lean on owners' words. The section is ready (*Clients' words*, hidden until you add quotes); add three real ones, with permission. 2. **A film.** A 20–40 second film of the atelier at work would make the opening (the banner accepts a video). 3. **Languages.** Italian and Russian clients are a large share; enable both in Shopify (Settings → Languages, with Translate & Adapt). |
